What Would You Do?

1You discover that your closest friend is cheating during an examination. Would you report it, confront your friend, or remain silent? Explain.

I would confront my closest friend first and tell them that what they're doing is wrong. Even though they're my friend, I don't think it's right to tolerate something dishonest just because of our relationship. I would encourage them to admit their mistake and take responsibility for their actions. For me, honesty should always come before friendship.

2You receive an expensive item that does not belong to you. Nobody knows that you have it. What would you do?

I would return the expensive item to its rightful owner, even if nobody knows that I have it. Keeping something that doesn't belong to me would only make me feel guilty. I believe that doing the right thing shouldn't depend on whether someone is watching. My principles should remain the same regardless of the situation.

3Your friends invite you to participate in an activity that goes against your personal values. How would you respond?

I would respectfully decline their invitation and explain why I don't want to participate. I don't think I should compromise my personal values just to fit in with my friends. Having friends doesn't mean I have to agree with everything they do. I'd rather stay true to myself than do something I might regret later.

4You are given an opportunity to become a leader, but you know that you lack confidence. Would you accept the responsibility?

I would accept the responsibility even though I lack confidence. I believe that confidence is something I can develop through experience and learning from my mistakes. Being a leader doesn't mean you have to be perfect from the beginning. Sometimes, you just have to take the opportunity and improve along the way.

5You witness someone being bullied online. What actions would you take?

I would stand up for the person being bullied and offer them my support. I would also report the situation to someone who can properly handle it instead of making things worse. I don't think staying silent is the right decision when someone is being treated unfairly. Even a simple act of support can make a difference.

6You accidentally damage something that belongs to another person. Nobody sees what happened. What would you do?

I would admit what happened and take responsibility for the damage. Even if nobody saw me, I know that I was responsible for it. I would apologize and find a way to make up for what I damaged. I believe that accountability matters more than avoiding the consequences of my actions.

7Your family wants you to pursue a career that is different from your dream profession. How would you handle the situation?

I would listen to my family's opinions while also explaining why I want to pursue my dream profession. I understand that they might have their own reasons and concerns about my future. However, I believe that I should also have a say in the life I'm going to build. I would try to find a way for us to understand each other's perspectives.

8You have limited money and must choose between buying something you want and something you need. How would you decide?

I would prioritize buying what I need rather than what I want. There are times when I have to understand that not everything I want is something I actually need. I would rather use my limited money wisely than spend it on something that only gives me temporary satisfaction. I can always buy what I want when I have enough to afford it.
